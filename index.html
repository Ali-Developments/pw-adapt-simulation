01 / THE CHALLENGE

Produced water is not a one-route problem.

Water characteristics can vary by source and operating conditions. A treatment sequence that works for one sample or end use may not be the best choice for another.

A
⌁

Variable water quality

Salinity, oil, suspended solids and other constituents can complicate treatment selection and monitoring.

B
⤳

Fixed routes have limits

A modular approach can help compare candidate treatment routes against the intended end use and operating constraints.

C
◎

Proof matters

Sensor readings and model outputs must be checked against laboratory evidence before performance claims are made.

Safety boundary

The prototype is not safety-rated industrial equipment. Hardware interlocks, containment, compatible wetted materials, supervised operation and an approved disposal route are prerequisites for real-water testing.

04 / EVIDENCE BEFORE CLAIMS

Validate the system—not just the interface.

Success should be demonstrated with repeatable tests and evidence traceable to the relevant measurement method.
